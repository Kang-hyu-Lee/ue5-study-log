# Exercises — Dot Product & Cross Product

Show every arithmetic step, like the worked examples in the Concepts file.
No calculator.

## 1. Dot product
A = (2, 3), B = (4, 1)
Compute A · B.

## 2. Dot product
A = (5, 0), B = (0, -5)
Compute A · B. What does the result tell you about the angle between them?

## 3. Dot product
A = (-2, -2), B = (2, 2)
Compute A · B. What does the sign tell you?

## 4. Cross product
A = (2, 3), B = (4, 1)
Compute A × B.

## 5. Cross product
Using the same A and B as #4, compute B × A.
Compare to your answer in #4 — what changed, and why?

## 6. Combined interpretation
A = (0, 5), B = (5, 5)
Compute both A · B and A × B.
In plain English: are they aligned? Which way would you rotate from A to
reach B?

## 7. Applied (gamedev framing)
A character is facing direction `forward = (1, 0)`. A target sits at
direction `toTarget = (0, -1)` relative to the character.
Compute `cross(forward, toTarget)` and `dot(forward, toTarget)`.
Should the character turn left or right to face the target? Is the
character currently facing toward it, away from it, or perpendicular to
it?
