# Day 1 Exercises — Algebra Basics & Order of Operations

Work these by hand, showing every step (like the worked example in the Concepts file). Don't skip to the answer — write out each PEMDAS step on its own line.

1. Evaluate: `6 + 2 × (5 − 3)³`

2. Evaluate: `(8 − 2) × 3 + 4²`

3. Evaluate: `20 ÷ 4 + 3 × (1 + 1)²`

4. Evaluate: `5² − (3 + 1) × 2 + 6 ÷ 3`

5. If `x = 4`, evaluate: `2x² + 3(x − 1)`
   *(here `2x²` means `2 × x²` — this is standard math shorthand: a number written directly next to a variable/parenthesis means multiply)*

6. Find the mistake: someone evaluated `2 + 3 × 4` as `20` (by doing `2 + 3` first, then `× 4`). Explain in one sentence why that's wrong, and give the correct answer.
